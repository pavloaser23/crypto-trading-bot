# 🚀 Getting Started

## Installation

1. Download the `.zip` archive from the Releases page
2. Extract the archive
3. Open the folder
4. Run the installer (`cryptobot-windows-x64-v1.0.exe`)
5. Complete installation

---

## First Launch

1. Open **CryptoBot**
2. Go to **Settings → Connections**
3. Connect your account (Exchange or Wallet)
4. Select a trading strategy
5. Click **Start**

---

## 🤖 Automation

Once started, CryptoBot trades automatically based on your selected strategy.

* No manual trading required
* Trades are executed automatically
* Risk management is applied in real-time

---

## Requirements

### 💻 System

* Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
* Minimum 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
* 435 MB free disk space

---

### 🌐 Network

* Stable internet connection
* Low latency recommended for trading
* No VPN restrictions (some exchanges may block access)

---

### 🔐 Accounts

* Exchange account (e.g. Binance, Bybit)
  
  **or**
* Crypto wallet (e.g. MetaMask, Trust Wallet)

---

### 🔑 Permissions

* API keys with **trading permissions only** (for exchanges)
* Withdrawal permissions should be **disabled**

---

### 🧠 Recommended Knowledge

* Basic understanding of trading
* Familiarity with risk management
* Knowledge of selected strategy

---

### ⚠️ Security

* Antivirus exceptions may be required
* Do not run the bot on public/shared computers
* Keep your credentials secure

---

## ⚠️ Notes

* Always extract the archive before running
* If Windows shows a warning → click **"More info" → "Run anyway"**
* Antivirus may block unknown `.exe` files
